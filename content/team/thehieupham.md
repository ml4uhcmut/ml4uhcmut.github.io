## About Me

### Introduction

Hello! I am **The Hieu Pham (Phạm Thế Hiểu)**, a Master's student in Computer Science at **Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (HCMUT), VNU-HCM**, and a researcher at the **Advanced Intelligence Technology Lab (AITech Lab)**. I received my Bachelor's degree in Computer Science through the Honor Program at HCMUT in 2025.

My research focuses on **speech and audio processing, generative modeling, and audio-language foundation models**. I am particularly interested in developing intelligent systems that can understand complex acoustic environments, reason over audio-grounded information, and interact proactively with humans.

Alongside my academic research, I work as an **Applied AI Scientist at Zalo**, contributing to speech and language technologies for the Kiki Auto voice assistant.

### Research Interests

My research interests lie at the intersection of **Speech Processing, Audio-Language Models, and Multimodal Intelligence**, with a particular focus on:

- **Generative Speech Enhancement:** Exploring generative models and discrete audio representations for high-quality speech restoration in challenging acoustic environments.
- **Target Speaker Extraction:** Developing robust, language-agnostic methods for isolating target speech from overlapping speakers and background interference.
- **Audio-Language Foundation Models:** Investigating how foundation models perceive, interpret, and reason about speech, environmental sounds, and complex acoustic scenes.
- **Proactive Multimodal Agents:** Building context-aware agents that can leverage auditory information to determine when and how to interact, moving beyond traditional command-following systems.

My long-term research goal is to develop **audio-grounded foundation models and proactive multimodal agents** capable of understanding real-world environments, identifying relevant contextual evidence, and adapting their behavior reliably.

My research spans both model development and empirical evaluation, including designing neural architectures, implementing training pipelines, constructing evaluation benchmarks, and analyzing model behavior under challenging acoustic conditions.

I also mentor undergraduate students on research and capstone projects involving speech processing, wake word detection, and agentic AI systems.

### Professional Experience

I am currently an **Applied AI Scientist at Zalo**, working on **Kiki Auto**, an in-car Vietnamese voice assistant.

My work involves:

- **Speech Technologies:** Developing and maintaining automatic speech recognition (ASR), text-to-speech (TTS), and on-device wake word detection systems.
- **Agentic AI and Language Understanding:** Developing LLM-based natural language understanding systems that interpret user intentions and translate them into executable actions.
- **Efficient AI Deployment:** Optimizing speech and language models for practical deployment in resource-constrained and real-world environments.

Previously, I worked as an **AI Software Engineer at Rennlabs**, where I developed AI-powered data pipelines, integrated large language and vision-language models into production systems, and built backend infrastructure for AI services.

My experience in both research and software engineering allows me to bridge the gap between **foundation model research and real-world AI applications**.
